How it works

From lock screen to desktop, without typing.

Thursday 24 September10:24
Enter Password
FinderFileEditViewGoWindowHelp
Thu 24 Sep 10:24

  1. 01Lock screen

    Your Mac locks. The notch opens.

    When the lock screen appears, Mac ID opens a small panel under the notch and starts looking for you.

  2. 02Recognition

    It recognises you, on the Mac.

    An ArcFace model running on the Apple Neural Engine matches your face. Typical recognition takes under a second — real logs show 70–900 ms. A liveness check turns away a printed photo or a photo on a phone screen.

  3. 03Password

    It types your password for you.

    macOS gives apps no way to approve a login, so Mac ID enters your password. It is stored AES-256-encrypted, and the key is released only by Touch ID or your Mac password.

  4. 04Desktop

    You’re in.

    If it doesn’t recognise you, nothing happens — the lock screen waits, and you type your password as usual.

Setup

Set up in four steps.

  1. 1

    Install and grant access

    Drag Mac ID to Applications. It asks for the camera, and for Accessibility so it can type your password at the lock screen.

  2. 2

    Enrol your face

    Follow the ring: look straight ahead, then turn slightly in each direction. Mac ID stores a mathematical description of your face — not a photograph.

  3. 3

    Paste your licence

    Settings → About → paste the key from your purchase email, and press Activate. During the 1-day trial you can skip this.

  4. 4

    Lock your Mac

    The notch opens, finds you, and types your password. If it doesn’t recognise you, nothing happens and you type it yourself.

Limits

Worth knowing before you install.

Mac ID is a convenience feature, not a security upgrade.

A MacBook camera sees a flat image, where an iPhone builds a 3D map of your face — so this cannot be as strong as Face ID or Touch ID. It reliably refuses a printed photo and a photo on a phone screen; it does not reliably refuse a video of you.

macOS gives no way for an app to authorise a login, so Mac ID unlocks by typing your stored password. That password is encrypted on your Mac behind Touch ID. If you are not comfortable with that trade, this is not the app for you.
